Forge support

How can
we help?

Email abelshelby95@gmail.com and we'll get back to you, usually within two business days.

Readiness shows a dash

Forge needs HRV, resting heart rate or sleep in Apple Health. Check the Health app → Profile → Apps → Forge and make sure reading is allowed. Oura users: turn on Apple Health sync in the Oura app. Readiness gets more accurate after about five days of data.

Coach isn't answering

AI coaching needs Forge Pro and your permission. Check Settings → AI coaching in Forge, and make sure you're online. Each account has a daily Coach limit that resets the next day.

A barcode isn't found

Forge looks products up in Open Food Facts, a free public database. If an item is missing, describe the meal or snap a photo instead.

Manage or cancel your subscription

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Forge. To cancel a free trial without being charged, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends.

Delete your data

In Forge, open Settings → Your data → Delete my data. This removes your account, your cloud backup and everything stored on your phone. Apple Health data isn't touched. You can also email us to request deletion.
