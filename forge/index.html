Forge for iPhone

A coach that knows
how you slept.

Forge reads your HRV, resting heart rate and sleep from Apple Health, then builds a workout that fits your recovery and the weights you actually lifted last time.

Built around your recovery

  • Daily readiness from Apple Health, including Oura and Apple Watch data
  • Workouts sized to your time, equipment and focus
  • Loads that progress from your logged sets

Log what you really lifted

  • Adjust weight and reps for every set in a couple of taps
  • A rest timer that alerts you even when you switch apps
  • Personal records and weekly volume, calculated from your sessions

Fuel that keeps up

  • Scan a barcode to log packaged food
  • Snap a photo or describe a meal for a calorie and protein estimate

Private by design

  • Forge only reads from Apple Health and never uses health data for ads
  • AI coaching is opt-in, and Forge shows exactly what it shares
  • Delete your account and data from Settings anytime
