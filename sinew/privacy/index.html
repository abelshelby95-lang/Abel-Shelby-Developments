Sinew Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

Sinew is a fitness and wellness app published by Abel Shelby Developments ("Sinew," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information Sinew uses, why, where it goes, and the choices you have.

The short version:

  • Sinew does not show ads, does not use third-party analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell your data.
  • Apple Health data is read only with your permission, is used only to power your coaching, and is never used for advertising, sold, or shared with data brokers.
  • When you use AI coaching features, the information needed for that request is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service to generate a response. Sinew asks your permission first, and you can turn it off at any time.

1. Information you enter

When you set up and use Sinew, you may enter:

  • Profile: your first name (optional, and kept only on your device — it is never uploaded or shared), goal, age, weight, height, and how many days a week you train.
  • Workout logs: exercises, sets, weights, and reps.
  • Meal logs: meal descriptions and the estimated calories and macros (protein, carbs, fat).
  • Bodyweight log: your weigh-ins over time.
  • Cardio and recovery: cardio sessions you log (type, time, distance, calories) and recovery sessions such as sauna or cold plunge (duration and temperature), plus your weekly goals.
  • Reminders and saved recipes: the reminders you set up and recipes you save.
  • Progress photos: photos you choose to take or add (see section 4 — they stay on your device).
  • Coach chat: the messages you send to Sinew's coach and the replies you receive.

We use this information to build your workouts, track your progress, estimate nutrition targets, and personalize coaching.

2. Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, Sinew reads the following from Apple Health:

  • Heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Sleep
  • Steps
  • Body weight
  • Workouts (for example runs, walks and rides recorded by Apple Watch or other apps), so they appear in your cardio log

Sinew only reads this data — it does not write to Apple Health. If you use an Oura ring or another wearable, its data reaches Sinew only through Apple Health; Sinew does not connect to Oura or any other wearable directly.

Sinew uses this data to calculate its own daily "readiness" estimate and to adjust your training and coaching. Sinew keeps a history of these daily readings so it can show you trends.

Our commitments about Apple Health data:

  • It is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • It is never sold.
  • It is never shared with data brokers, advertising platforms, or analytics providers.
  • It is used only to provide Sinew's health and fitness features to you. A summary of it may be included in AI coaching requests you choose to make (see section 3).

You can change which data Sinew can read at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices → Sinew.

3. AI coaching (Anthropic's Claude)

Sinew's Coach chat, workout builder, and meal analysis are powered by Claude, an AI service provided by Anthropic. When you use one of these features, Sinew sends the information needed for that request to Anthropic so Claude can generate a response. Depending on the feature, this can include:

  • Profile stats (age, weight, goal) and your calorie and protein targets
  • A summary of your readiness, HRV, and sleep
  • Your recent lift history (exercise names, weights, reps, dates)
  • The meal description you typed, or the meal photo you took
  • Your chat messages, including earlier messages in the conversation
  • A short summary of your recent cardio and recovery sessions
  • For recipe ideas: your goal, the calories and protein you have left for the day, and any preferences you type

Your permission comes first. Sinew asks for your explicit permission in the app before sending any of your data to Anthropic. You can turn AI coaching off at any time in Sinew's Settings. With it off, no data is sent to Anthropic, and the AI features won't work.

Anthropic handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy. According to Anthropic's commercial terms, data sent through its API is not used to train its models by default.

These requests pass through Sinew's server (a function hosted by our infrastructure provider, Supabase) on their way to Anthropic, so the AI service key never lives inside the app. That server checks you're signed in to Sinew and counts requests to enforce a daily limit; it does not store the content of your requests or Claude's replies.

4. Camera, photos, barcodes, and notifications

Sinew uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to:

  • Take or add progress photos. Progress photos are stored only on your device, inside Sinew's private storage. They are never uploaded, backed up to our servers, shared, or sent to any AI service. Deleting Sinew, or using Delete my data, permanently deletes them.
  • Scan a food barcode. The camera reads the barcode on the device. Sinew sends only the barcode number to Open Food Facts, a free public food database (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), to look up the product's nutrition. No personal information is sent with it, and this doesn't use AI coaching.
  • Photograph a meal for macro estimation. The photo is sent to Anthropic for analysis (see section 3). Sinew saves the resulting estimate (meal name, calories, macros), not the photo itself.

If you allow notifications, Sinew schedules reminders on your phone: "rest is up" alerts during workouts, recovery timer alerts, and any reminders you set up yourself. These are created on your device and aren't sent through any server.

5. Where your data is stored

Your profile, logs, chat history, and health readings are stored on your device.

Cloud backup: Sinew automatically creates an anonymous account for your install (no email or password) and backs up your profile numbers, workout, meal, cardio and recovery logs, reminders, saved recipes, chat history, and daily health readings to a hosted database run by our infrastructure provider, Supabase, so your data isn't lost. Your first name and progress photos are not included. Each account can only access its own data. The provider stores data on our behalf and may not use it for its own purposes. Data is encrypted in transit.

6. Your Sinew account

You don't need to create an account or give us an email address. Sinew automatically creates an anonymous account the first time you open the app. It's identified only by a random ID and is used to back up your data and to count AI coaching requests toward the daily limit. If we add optional sign-in (such as Sign in with Apple) in the future, we'll update this policy first.

7. Sinew Pro subscription

Sinew Pro (7-day free trial, then $12.99 per month or $79.99 per year) is billed through Apple's App Store. Apple handles all payments. Sinew never sees or stores your card or payment details. We receive only information needed to confirm your subscription is active (for example, subscription status and renewal date). Manage or cancel your subscription in the iPhone Settings app under your Apple ID → Subscriptions.

8. What we don't do

  • No advertising, in the app or using your data.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No tracking you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting your personal information to anyone.

9. Who we share data with

We share data only:

  • With Anthropic, when you use AI coaching features and have given permission (section 3).
  • With our infrastructure provider, Supabase, which stores your backup and runs Sinew's server on our behalf (sections 3 and 5).
  • With Apple, as needed to process App Store subscriptions (section 7).
  • If required by law, such as a valid court order, and only what is required.
  • With Open Food Facts, only the barcode number when you scan a product (section 4).

10. Deleting your data

  • On your device: deleting the Sinew app removes the data stored on your device.
  • Apple Health: deleting Sinew doesn't delete anything from Apple Health; that data belongs to you in the Health app.
  • In-app account deletion: you can delete your Sinew account from within the app in Settings → Your data → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account and the data stored in our cloud database.
  • By email: you can also ask us to delete your data by writing to abelshelby95@gmail.com.

Data already sent to Anthropic for an AI request is handled under Anthropic's retention policy (see section 3).

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, download, or delete your personal information, or to object to how it's used. Most of your data can be viewed, edited, or deleted directly in the app. For anything else, contact us at abelshelby95@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time and as required by law.

12. Children

Sinew is not directed to children. It is not intended for anyone under 13, or under 16 in the European Union and UK. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact abelshelby95@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

13. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypting data in transit. No app or service can guarantee perfect security, so please keep your device protected with a passcode.

14. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the effective date above. If a change is significant — for example, a new way your data is shared — we'll let you know in the app before it takes effect.
